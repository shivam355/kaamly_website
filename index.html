Why Kyrodi

Built for your society, not the whole city.

Big platforms send whoever's nearest. Kyrodi works with familiar, trusted people who already serve your community, so every visit feels like a neighbour helping out.

How It Works

Booked in under a minute. Done without a thought.

  1. 01

    Open Kyrodi

    Tap the link or the Kyrodi icon on your home screen. No download, no sign-up hassle.

  2. 02

    Choose a service

    Pick a vehicle wash or ironing and add any special requests.

  3. 03

    Pick a time

    Choose a slot that suits your day. Your trusted provider takes it from there.

  4. 04

    Sit back & relax

    It's handled at your doorstep while you get on with your day.
